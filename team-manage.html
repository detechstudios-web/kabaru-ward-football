🔁 Transfer rule: A transfer is only a request at this stage. The current team must review it first, followed by final administrator approval. The player is not moved until all required approvals are completed.
🏟️ Club Squad: Select All Competitions — Club Squad when you want to manage the player's club relationship rather than a single competition. Competition registration remains separate, so the same club player can be eligible for multiple competitions without creating duplicate player records.
🚪 Release rule: Releasing a player creates a pending Free Agent request. The player is not released until the administrator approves it. Approved release removes the player from the current club squad while preserving the player's profile, statistics, and club history.
